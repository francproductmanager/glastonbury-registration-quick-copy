Glastonbury 2027 resale: date, rules and how to get ready

Everything known about the Glastonbury 2027 resale: when it's likely to be, the registration deadline, how many tickets per booking, paying in full, and how to get your group ready.

By the maker of Glasto Quick Copy (about us). Facts checked 26 October 2026.

If you missed out in October, it isn't necessarily over. Tickets that aren't paid for in the April balance window, and tickets that are returned, are sold again in the official spring resale. It's smaller and faster than the main sale, and it works a little differently, so it's worth preparing for on its own terms. We update this page as soon as the festival announces anything new.

Until the festival publishes the 2027 details, the 2025 resale below is the best guide to how it will run. To have the date land in your calendar the moment we add it, subscribe to the 2027 dates calendar. It needs no sign-up and no email address.

Where resale tickets come from

After the April balance payment window closes, any ticket whose balance wasn't paid is cancelled. Those tickets, plus tickets that were cancelled or returned since the main sale, go back on sale. The festival doesn't say in advance how many there will be, and demand is always far higher than supply.

When it happens

Usually in late April, a couple of weeks after the balance window. In recent years there have been two sessions: one for ticket and coach packages, and one for general admission. In 2025 they were:

The 2027 resale date hasn't been announced yet. See Glastonbury 2027 key dates.

How the resale queue worked in 2025

  • Everyone already on the page when the sale opened was placed in the queue at random. Anyone arriving later went to the back.
  • A progress bar showed your position. The advice was not to refresh, and not to use several devices or tabs.
  • At the front, you had 10 minutes to complete the page with everyone's registration details. Tickets weren't allocated until payment went through, and reports said the payment page had its own, shorter timer.
  • Registration numbers could be locked for up to 10 minutes after errors, duplicate attempts or a timeout.
  • If the page hung, the advice was to press back once, and if that didn't work, close the browser and start again.

Getting your group ready

  • Check everyone's registration is still valid and the photo still looks like them. The months between the main sale and the resale are a good time to update it, before registration closes again.
  • Plan in fours, not sixes. Decide in advance who books whom, because a group of six needs two bookings.
  • Have the full price ready for each person you might book for. Four tickets at the 2027 price is over £1,600, so check your card limit. See paying for Glastonbury tickets.
  • Only book people who still need tickets. In a mixed group, some people already have tickets from the main sale. Anyone with a registration can queue, but only those without tickets need booking.
  • Keep your group's page. If you used Glasto Quick Copy for the main sale, groups are cleared on a seasonal schedule, so check yours is still saved or set it up again before the resale.

For the queue itself, the same rules apply as in the main sale: see how the queue works. For paying in full, see the checkout guide.

Coach packages in the resale

Ticket and coach packages come back in their own resale session, usually a few days before general admission, limited to two per booking. The coach rules still apply: you must travel on your booked coach and can't switch to general admission. See coach tickets.

Avoid unofficial sellers

Glastonbury tickets carry the holder's photo and are strictly non-transferable. The official resale through See Tickets is the only legitimate way to get a returned ticket. Anyone selling tickets elsewhere is either mistaken or a scam. See how to spot Glastonbury ticket scams.

Keep your registration photo up to date between sales, so you're ready if a resale comes round.
