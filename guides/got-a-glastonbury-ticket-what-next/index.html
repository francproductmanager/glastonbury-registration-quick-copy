Glastonbury 2027 balance payment and what to do before June

The 2027 balance window (1 to 7 April), how paying it works, parking and protection, and a month by month list of everything else between booking and the festival, including when tickets arrive.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 26 October 2026.

4 min read

Getting through the queue is the hard part, but a deposit isn't a ticket yet. The one deadline that matters is the balance payment window: 9am on Thursday 1 April to 11.59pm on Wednesday 7 April 2027, UK time. Any ticket whose balance isn't paid by then is cancelled. This guide covers that week first, then everything else between now and June.

Add the window to your calendar now. The file opens in your calendar app, with a reminder the day before:

  • Add to calendar: Glastonbury 2027 balance payment window

The festival and See Tickets send reminders, but paying on time is each ticket holder's responsibility. In 2025 the balance page also let people change the delivery address for their tickets. What happens to the deposit if a balance isn't paid, and what to do if someone can't go, is in deposits and balance payments.

Ask each person to reply in the group chat with "PAID" once their balance is done. The lead booker can then see at a glance who still needs reminding before the 7th.

Parking and protection

For 2027, car park passes are sold during the balance window, for general admission ticket holders only. The festival's information page listed them at £60, plus £3 postage where bought separately when we checked on 26 October 2026, so check that page again before you pay. They can't be added to a ticket and coach booking. Decide as a group who's buying parking before the window opens, so you don't end up with two passes or none. More on passes, and on driving there, is in Glastonbury car parking.

Campervan pitches, Worthy View and tipis are sold separately: see campervans, camping and parking.

Cancellation protection can also be added while you pay. Read what it covers before buying it: it refunds in certain circumstances, not for any change of plan. The refund deadline and charge are in Glastonbury ticket refunds.

Straight after booking

  • Keep the confirmation. Save the confirmation email and screenshot the order reference. Forward it to the group.
  • Tell everyone they're booked, and who booked them. People need to know whose booking they're in for the balance and for any questions later.
  • Settle the deposits between you. See the syndicate guide for a simple way to split them.

If someone needs access facilities

There are no separate access tickets: everyone books the normal way, then applies for access facilities after getting a ticket. For 2027 the link to the Access Application Form is available from the end of November 2026, and you request it from the festival's Accessibility Team. Facilities can reach capacity, so apply early. See going to Glastonbury with access requirements.

Over the winter

  • Check the card you'll pay the balance with won't expire before April.
  • Start putting money aside. The 2027 balance is £308 per ticket plus postage. From October to early April that's about six months, or roughly £52 a month. For the whole trip, see what Glastonbury 2027 costs.
  • Decide how you're getting there. Coach places only come as ticket and coach packages in their own sale, not as add-ons to a general admission ticket. See coach tickets.
  • Keep your email address working. Reminders and updates go to the email on your booking and registration.

Your photo is now locked

Once a booking is linked to your registration, you can't change your photo. If your appearance changes a lot before June, it's worth contacting See Tickets for advice rather than assuming it'll be fine at the gate, because the ticket shows your registered photo.

In the spring

  • If some of your group still need tickets, get ready for the 2027 resale.
  • Watch for the line-up announcements and the festival's information on travel, camping and what you can bring.
  • Make sure the delivery address for your tickets is right.

When do tickets arrive?

General admission tickets are posted to the address on the booking, and coach passengers are given their festival tickets on the coach. The festival hasn't announced 2027 delivery dates yet. For an earlier festival, See Tickets said to expect tickets at least 14 days before the festival started. If yours haven't arrived by then, contact See Tickets customer service.

Keep everyone's tickets safe, and agree how they'll get to each person before travel day. Each ticket shows its holder's photo, so you can't swap tickets between yourselves.

Add the balance week to your calendar

Friends still without a ticket? The resale is their next chance, and whoever gets through needs everyone's details to hand: get their resale group ready.
