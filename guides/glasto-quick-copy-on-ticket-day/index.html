Using Glasto Quick Copy at the front of the queue

How to get from "you're through" to a paid booking quickly with Glasto Quick Copy: getting every queuer the page, switching between screens, pasting on a phone and a timed practice run.

By the maker of Glasto Quick Copy (about us). Facts checked 26 October 2026.

When you reach the front of the Glastonbury queue, the clock starts. The 2027 sale FAQ said you'd have 10 minutes on the page where you enter the registration details, and tickets aren't allocated until payment has gone through. Entering six registration numbers and six postcodes is the part of that time you can make fast.

This guide is about those few minutes: getting up to six people's details from your group's page into the booking form without a typo. Setting the page up in the first place is covered in how to use Glasto Quick Copy.

Before the day: every queuer needs the page

Whoever gets through first does the booking, and you can't know in advance who that will be. So everyone who's queueing for your group needs the same page, on the device they'll queue on.

  • Send the link to every queuer. Use Share link or Copy link on your group's page. Opening it saves a copy on their phone.
  • Ask each of them to open it on the device they'll queue on, and to check the names look right.
  • Send a new link if anything changes. Each phone keeps its own copy, so a corrected postcode only reaches people who open the new link.
  • More than six people? Save each booking group as its own group, named so it's obvious, and send both links to every queuer. Then whoever gets through opens the group they've agreed to book.

Get the two screens close together

The move you'll make again and again is: copy on your group's page, switch to the booking page, paste, switch back. Make that switch as short as possible.

  • On a phone: open your group's page in your browser before you join the queue, so you're switching to something already open rather than opening a new tab during checkout. On an iPhone without a Home button, swiping right along the bottom edge jumps back to the last app.
  • On Android: if your phone supports split screen, you can have the booking page and your group's page on screen together, with no switching at all.
  • On a laptop: open your group's page on the laptop too, in its own window next to the booking page. Copying on your phone won't paste on your laptop.

Our guide to setting up your phone or laptop has the settings for each.

At the front: one person at a time

  1. Post in the group chat that you're through and who you're booking.
  2. Open the group you're booking. The first box to copy is highlighted.
  3. Tap the registration number. It's copied.
  4. Switch to the booking page, press and hold in the registration number box, and tap Paste.
  5. Switch back, tap the postcode, and paste that too.
  6. Check the name the booking page shows is the person you expect, then move on to the next person.

Doing each person's number and postcode together, then checking their name, means a mistake shows up straight away, while you still know which person it is. The next box to copy is highlighted after each tap, and a progress bar shows how many you've done, so you don't lose your place if you're interrupted.

Pasting on a phone

On most phones, press and hold inside the box on the booking form, then tap Paste. Many keyboards also show the last thing you copied as a suggestion above the keys, which you can tap instead. That's often the fastest way of all.

If a copy doesn't work

  • A box says to tap again. Your browser didn't allow the copy. Tap it once more.
  • It still won't copy. The number and postcode are shown in full on the box, so you can type them in. Read it back carefully before moving on.
  • A name doesn't come up on the booking page. The details may be wrong in the group itself. Don't keep retrying: carry on with the others and check that person in the chat. See what to do if checkout fails.

Keep screen on

Turn on Keep screen on on your group's page and the tool asks your browser to stop the screen locking while the page is open, if your browser supports it. It switches off when you leave the page, so while you're on the booking page your phone's own screen lock setting is what counts. Set that to a longer time before the sale.

A timed practice run

The practice demo has a group of made-up people and saves nothing. Use it a few days before the sale:

  1. Open the demo, and open a notes app or a message to yourself as a stand-in for the booking form.
  2. Start a timer.
  3. Copy and paste each person's registration number and postcode into the note, switching back and forth as you would on the day. The demo group has four people; a real booking can have up to six.
  4. Stop the timer and check every number pasted in full.
  5. Do it again. The second go is almost always much quicker, because the switching becomes automatic.

Never practise on the real See Tickets site, and never with the queue open. A practice run is for your hands, not for the ticket site.

What the tool can't do

It's worth being clear. Glasto Quick Copy can't speed up the queue or get you a better place in it. It doesn't connect to See Tickets and doesn't fill in the booking form for you. It never sends your group's details anywhere. What it does is make the minute after you get through calm and accurate, which is the part of ticket day you can control.
