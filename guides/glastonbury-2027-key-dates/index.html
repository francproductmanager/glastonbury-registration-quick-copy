Glastonbury 2027: key dates, prices and what happens next

Every confirmed date for Glastonbury 2027 in one place: the sales that have happened, the balance window, access applications, the resale and the festival itself.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 29 October 2026.

3 min read

Glastonbury took 2026 as a fallow year, so 2027 is the first festival since 2025. Tickets went on sale in October 2026, earlier in the autumn than the November sales of recent years, and sold out in 42 minutes. This page brings together every confirmed date and price, and what's still to be announced. We update it as the festival publishes new information.

If you got a ticket

Your next deadline is the balance window from 1 to 7 April 2027: every ticket that isn't paid for by then is cancelled. How to pay, and what happens if someone can't, is in deposits and balance payments. If anyone in your booking needs access facilities, the application form opens at the end of November and places can run out, so don't leave it. Our guide to paying the balance and what to do before June goes through everything else.

If you missed out

Tickets that aren't fully paid for in April, and tickets that are returned, go back on sale in the spring resale. The 2027 date hasn't been announced. In 2025 the coach resale was on a Thursday evening and the general admission resale on the following Sunday morning, both in late April. See how the spring resale works, when the next ticket sale is and other ways to get to Glastonbury. If you live in the Mendip and Central Somerset area, Sunday tickets go on sale at 11am on Sunday 1 November 2026.

Who needs a ticket in 2027

Everyone who will be 13 or over on Wednesday 23 June 2027 needs a ticket and a registration. In practice that means anyone born on or before 23 June 2014. Children younger than that don't need a ticket. See taking children to Glastonbury.

What's still to come

  • The exact date and time of the spring resale.
  • The exact date registration reopens for new registrations and photo updates. The festival says it is suspended before and during the local sale on Sunday 1 November 2026, and reopens later in the autumn.
  • More information on accessibility, coaches, campervans, parking and accommodation, which the festival has said is coming soon.

Tickets are only ever sold at glastonbury.seetickets.com. Anyone else offering 2027 tickets is not an official seller. See how to spot Glastonbury ticket scams.

Still hoping for a ticket? A resale booking can cover up to four people (in 2025), and whoever gets through first needs everyone's registration number and postcode to hand.
