How to get to Glastonbury Festival: train, bus, bike and drop off

Getting to Glastonbury 2027 without a car: the train to Castle Cary and free shuttle bus, local buses, cycling, the Drop and Collect point and private coaches.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 12 November 2026.

6 min read

Glastonbury 2027 runs Wednesday 23 to Sunday 27 June 2027 at Worthy Farm in Somerset. Without a car, the main routes are the train to Castle Cary, then the festival's free shuttle bus to the gate; a coach; a local bus; your bike; or a lift to the festival's Drop and Collect point. The festival says over a third of ticket holders travel by coach, train or other public transport, and it encourages more to. This guide covers each route, using its information page. Coach packages and driving have their own guides, linked below.

By train to Castle Cary

The festival says the best station for the festival is Castle Cary. There are trains from Bristol Temple Meads, sometimes with a change at Westbury, and that journey takes around 70 minutes.

  • Book early. Cheaper Advance tickets go on sale up to 12 weeks ahead, which for late June means early April.
  • Save a third with a Railcard, or with GroupSave on some journeys if three or more of you travel together.
  • Leave the trolley at home. The festival says train companies won't allow trolleys or carts on their trains during the festival.
  • Don't plan to park or leave bags at the station. There's no parking at Castle Cary during the festival and no left luggage.
  • Print your e-ticket too. There's nowhere to charge your phone at the station, so the festival suggests a printed copy as a backup.

The free shuttle bus from Castle Cary

A free shuttle runs between Castle Cary station and the Festival Bus Station, by Gate A, for festival ticket holders. You need your festival ticket to use it. It runs from Wednesday to Monday, but not at night when there are no trains, and trolleys aren't allowed on it. The festival hasn't published 2027 times yet. Getting the train home

On the Sunday and Monday a bus also runs from the Festival Bus Station to Bristol Temple Meads. The festival says buses back to both stations are timed to arrive before the last train, so don't head to the station outside those times: you'd wait in a car park with no facilities. It's better to wait in the bus park, which has shelter, food and drink, and catch the first bus in the morning. Extra trains run, but expect queues. The festival says trains on Sunday afternoon and in the early hours of Monday tend to be less crowded. If you're leaving on the Monday with a Saver ticket, don't arrive too early, or you may be asked to pay more or have to wait for the right train.

By local bus

The festival lists the local buses it ran to the Festival Bus Station in 2025, and says 2027 services are yet to be confirmed. In 2025:

  • From Glastonbury town, the festival bus GF1 ran from Wednesday to Monday, calling at West Pennard. Single fares were £4.50 from Glastonbury and £3.50 from West Pennard.
  • From Bristol, the 374, 375 or 376 ran from Bristol Temple Meads to Glastonbury town, to connect with GF1.
  • From Bath, the 174 ran to Shepton Mallet, to connect with the festival bus GF2, which ran from Wells via Shepton Mallet and Cannard's Grave.

By coach

The festival's official coach partner runs services from over 90 places around the UK, and you can book a seat with a general admission ticket. Ticket and coach packages are a separate thing, sold in their own sale. Both, with the other coach companies, are in Glastonbury coach tickets.

By bike

The festival calls cycling the most sustainable way to get there. If you arrive by bike:

  • Free, secure bike parks. There are two, one on the west side of the site and one on the east.
  • A cyclists only campsite with showers, by Gate A. There's no need to book, but only people who actually arrived by bike can use it.
  • No cycling on site. The festival's terms say ticket holders can't ride bikes on the site.
  • Help with luggage. Bike To Glasto, which runs the cycling facilities, offers luggage transport for a small fee, with drop off points around Somerset and one in west London, or a courier option.

Being dropped off: Drop and Collect

If someone is giving you a lift but not staying, the festival asks them not to drop you on local roads or near the vehicle gates, which it says is dangerous. Use the Drop and Collect point instead:

  • Where: east of the site. Head towards Evercreech on the A371 and follow the signs for Drop and Collect, or DC.
  • How you get in: a bus runs around the clock to Gate A.
  • When: from 9am on the day before the festival starts (for 2027, Tuesday 22 June) until 8pm on the day after it ends.
  • What's there: toilets and a burger van.
  • What can't come: large wheelbarrows, wheelie bins and trolleys aren't taken to the site.

For the pick up after the festival, plan a route that avoids the area right around the site: the festival says the A361 is closed to through traffic on the Monday after the festival, between Glastonbury and Shepton Mallet. Minibuses with fewer than 15 seats use Drop and Collect too.

Private hire coaches

A group hiring its own coach of 15 seats or more drops off and picks up at a dedicated area on site, then walks to the main pedestrian gate. The coach needs a Private Hire Coach Park pass. That cost £170 including VAT in 2025, and the festival hasn't announced the 2027 price. To book one for 2027, the festival asks you to use its contact form, choosing "Any Other Enquiries", with your preferred arrival time on Wednesday 23 June and departure time on Monday 28 June, the lead booker's details, an on site phone number, the coach company, the number of passengers and, if you know it, the vehicle registration.

Driving yourself

Everything about driving there is in Glastonbury car parking. The festival also says it doesn't endorse or facilitate travel by helicopter, except for operational purposes.

A tip for the way home

Whether you're going home by train or coach, the festival suggests printing a spare copy of your return ticket and leaving it in one of the free property lock-ups when you arrive. If you lose your phone or ticket, or your battery dies, you can collect it and still get home. Allow time on the last day to collect anything you've left in a lock-up before you queue for the bus.

What we couldn't confirm

The festival hasn't published 2027 times for the Castle Cary shuttle, the local buses or the Bristol bus, so the times and fares above are from 2025. We'll update this guide when the 2027 details appear. If you have access requirements, the festival runs a wheelchair accessible shuttle bus for access customers: see Glastonbury 2027 accessibility.
