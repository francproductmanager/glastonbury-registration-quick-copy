How fast Glastonbury tickets sell out: recent sales compared

Sell-out times, prices and deposits for the last three Glastonbury ticket sales and resales, and what the numbers mean for your group.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 30 October 2026.

2 min read

Every Glastonbury sale in recent years has sold out in under an hour. Looking at the last few side by side shows how quickly things move, how the price has changed, and why the minutes straight after you get through the queue matter so much.

Spring resales

The resale is much smaller than the main sale, because it only offers tickets that weren't paid for or were returned. It also tends to go faster: the 2024 general admission resale sold out in about 22 minutes and the 2025 one in about 19 minutes.

What the numbers tell us

The price is rising by 5% to 8% a sale

From £360 to £378.50 is a rise of about 5.1%. From £378.50 to £408 is about 7.8%. The deposit went up by a third, from £75 to £100, so the lead booker for a group of six now needs £600 available on ticket day, up from £450.

The window is short and getting no longer

Somewhere between 20 and 60 minutes is all there is. For 2027, a buyer on the fan subreddit reported completing a purchase at 9.04am, the queue page showed over half the tickets gone by 9.26am, and See Tickets confirmed the sell-out at 9.42am. A booking that takes an extra three minutes because someone is hunting for a friend's number is a real cost.

The queue keeps changing

Every sale since 2025 has used a new kind of queue, and 2027 changed it again. Expect the process to keep evolving and read the official instructions before every sale. How it works now is in how the queue works.

What this means for your group

  • Nothing you do changes your place in the queue. Preparation is about the minutes after it.
  • The clock at the front is short. Six registration numbers and postcodes fit comfortably in it if they're ready to paste, and not if they're buried in a chat. See the checkout.
  • More people queueing gives more chances. Every member of your group with their own registration can try, as long as you've agreed who books whom.
  • Budget for the deposit early, because it has gone up and could again.
