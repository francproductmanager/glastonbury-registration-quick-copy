When is the next Glastonbury ticket sale?

The next Glastonbury ticket sales, what's been announced, when recent sales have happened, and the steps to take now so your group is ready.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 29 October 2026.

3 min read

For most people, the next chance to buy Glastonbury tickets is the 2027 spring resale, after the April balance window. After that comes the main sale for 2028. Neither date has been announced. Before either, there's a sale only for people who live near the festival: local full weekend and Sunday tickets on Sunday 1 November 2026. This page always covers the next sale, and we update it the day the festival announces anything.

There was no 2026 festival, so no sale for it. The 2027 sale came earlier in the autumn than the November sales before it. Registration has closed a week or so before each sale, so watch for that date as much as the sale itself. Sell-out times are in how fast Glastonbury tickets sell out.

What to expect at the next sale

Each sale has had its own official FAQ, and the festival has changed the process before, so read the new one when it's published. Based on the most recent sales:

Resale (as in 2025)Main sale (as in 2027)
Tickets per bookingUp to 4 general admission, or 2 ticket and coach packagesUp to 6
PaymentThe full price, at bookingA deposit (£100 a ticket in 2027), then the balance in April
RegistrationEveryone in the bookingEveryone in the booking, and each queuer entered their own registration before joining the queue
QueueRandom places for everyone waiting when it opensOne place per registration, so more people queueing means more chances

The details that cost groups their chance are the same every time: a registration that wasn't accepted, a postcode that doesn't match, numbers buried in a chat, and a card that can't cover the payment. All of them can be sorted weeks before any date is announced, which is why the list below starts now rather than when the sale does.

What to do now

  1. Check every registration in your group, and update old photos while registration is open. See how to register.
  2. Agree your syndicate: who's going, who'll queue, and who books whom. See the syndicate guide.
  3. Collect everyone's details in one place. See the spreadsheet template.
  4. Subscribe to the dates calendar, below, so new sale dates reach you as soon as we add them.
