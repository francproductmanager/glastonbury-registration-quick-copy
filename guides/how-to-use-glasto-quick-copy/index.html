How to use Glasto Quick Copy

A step-by-step guide to putting your group on one page, sharing it, using it on ticket day and fixing common problems.

By the maker of Glasto Quick Copy (about us). Facts checked 20 October 2026.

Glasto Quick Copy is a free tool that puts your whole group's registration numbers and postcodes on one page, so on ticket day you can tap to copy each one and paste it straight into the booking form.

You don't need an account or an app. It works in the browser on your phone or laptop. You can try it first with made-up people in the practice demo.

1. Paste your group chat

Open the tool and choose Set up your group. Paste the messages where your friends sent their details, then choose Review details. The tool picks out names, registration numbers and postcodes for you.

It understands messages copied from WhatsApp on iPhone and Android, including the date, time and sender at the start of each line, and it ignores chat like "thanks" or "done". It works best when each person's details are on one line, for example Alex 1029384756 BS1 4DJ (made-up details).

Prefer typing? Choose Enter details manually and fill in each person.

2. Check and save

Check each person's name, number and postcode against what they sent. Fix anything that's wrong, add anyone who's missing, and fill in any postcode the tool couldn't find. Then choose Save group. It's saved in your browser on your phone. There's no account to make.

The pasted text itself is never stored, only the details you saved.

3. Send everyone the link

Use Share link or Copy link to send the page to your group. Opening it saves an editable copy on their phones, ready to use. The link contains first names, registration numbers and postcodes only, so keep it within the group.

Each phone keeps its own copy. If you change someone's details after sharing, send the new link so everyone has the latest version.

4. On ticket day

When you get through the queue, open your group. Tap a box to copy it, paste it into the booking form, and move on. The next box to copy is highlighted, a progress bar counts what's done, and you can turn on Keep screen on so your phone doesn't lock. For a step by step run through the booking itself, and a timed practice, see using Glasto Quick Copy at the front of the queue.

Pasting on a phone

On most phones, press and hold inside the box on the booking form, then tap Paste. Some keyboards also show the last thing you copied as a suggestion above the keys, which you can tap instead.

Using a laptop and a phone together

Some people queue on a laptop and keep the group's page open on their phone. That works, but copying on the phone won't paste on the laptop. Open the group's page on the same device you're booking on: send yourself the link, and open it there.

If something goes wrong

  • A box says to tap again. The browser didn't allow the copy. Tap it once more. If it still fails, the number is shown in full on the box, so you can type it in.
  • A friend's link doesn't open. Links stop working after the seasonal clear-by date. Set the group up again and send a new link.
  • Your group has gone. Clearing your browser's data, using a private window or the seasonal clearing will remove it. Open a share link again, or set it up again.

Privacy

The tool runs in your browser. Your group is saved on your phone only, and the tool's code never sends it anywhere. The site is free because it shows Google ads, so Google's ad code also runs on these pages. The code is open source, and a public check compares the live site with the published code every six hours.

Groups and links are cleared on a seasonal schedule, so details don't hang around after ticket season. If you want to delete everything sooner, open How your data is handled in the tool and choose Delete everything on this phone.
