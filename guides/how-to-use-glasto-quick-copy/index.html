How to use Glasto Quick Copy, from setup to ticket day

A step-by-step guide to putting your group on one page, sharing it with everyone who's queueing, filling in the booking form at the front of the queue, a timed practice run, and fixing common problems.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 26 October 2026.

6 min read

Glasto Quick Copy is a free tool that puts your whole group's registration numbers and postcodes on one page, so when you get through the Glastonbury queue you can tap to copy each one and paste it straight into the booking form.

You don't need an account or an app. It works in the browser on your phone or laptop. You can try it first with made-up people in the practice demo. This guide covers everything: setting up, sharing, the booking itself and what to do if something goes wrong.

1. Paste your group chat

Open the tool and choose Set up your group. Paste the messages where your friends sent their details, then choose Review details. The tool picks out names, registration numbers and postcodes for you.

It understands messages copied from WhatsApp on iPhone and Android, including the date, time and sender at the start of each line, and it ignores chat like "thanks" or "done". It also reads rows copied from a spreadsheet. It works best when each person's details are together, for example Alex 1029384756 BS1 4DJ (made-up details). For how to collect the details in the first place, see collecting registration numbers from WhatsApp.

Prefer typing? Choose Enter details manually and fill in each person.

2. Check and save

Check each person's name, number and postcode against what they sent. Fix anything that's wrong, add anyone who's missing, and fill in any postcode the tool couldn't find. Then choose Save group. It's saved in your browser on your phone. The pasted text itself is never stored, only the details you saved.

A group holds up to six people, the most one booking can cover. For a bigger syndicate, save each booking group as its own group.

3. Send everyone the link

Whoever gets through first does the booking, and you can't know in advance who that will be. So everyone who's queueing needs the page, on the device they'll queue on.

  • Use Share link or Copy link on your group's page and send it to every queuer. Opening it saves a copy on their phone.
  • Ask each of them to open it on the device they'll queue on, and to check the names look right.
  • Send a new link if anything changes. Each phone keeps its own copy, so a corrected postcode only reaches people who open the new link.
  • More than one group? Send every queuer the link for each group, so whoever gets through can open the one they've agreed to book.

The link contains first names, registration numbers and postcodes only, so keep it within the group.

4. At the front of the queue

When you reach the front, the booking page opens with a timer, and tickets aren't allocated until payment has gone through. Entering six registration numbers and six postcodes is the part of that time you can make fast.

  1. Post in the group chat that you're through and who you're booking.
  2. Open the group you're booking. The first box to copy is highlighted.
  3. Tap the registration number. It's copied.
  4. Switch to the booking page, press and hold in the registration number box, and tap Paste.
  5. Switch back, tap the postcode, and paste that too.
  6. Check the name the booking page shows is the person you expect, then move on to the next person.

Doing each person's number and postcode together, then checking their name, means a mistake shows up straight away, while you still know which person it is. The next box to copy is highlighted after each tap, and a progress bar shows how many you've done, so you don't lose your place if you're interrupted. The timers and everything else about paying are in the checkout guide.

Get the two screens close together

  • On a phone: open your group's page in your browser before you join the queue, so you're switching to something already open rather than opening a new tab during checkout. On an iPhone without a Home button, swiping right along the bottom edge jumps back to the last app.
  • On Android: if your phone supports split screen, you can have the booking page and your group's page on screen together.
  • On a laptop: open your group's page on the laptop too, in its own window next to the booking page. Copying on your phone won't paste on your laptop.

Pasting on a phone

On most phones, press and hold inside the box on the booking form, then tap Paste. Many keyboards also show the last thing you copied as a suggestion above the keys, which you can tap instead. That's often the fastest way of all.

Keep screen on

Turn on Keep screen on on your group's page and the tool asks your browser to stop the screen locking while the page is open, if your browser supports it. It switches off when you leave the page, so while you're on the booking page your phone's own screen lock setting is what counts. See phone or laptop: setting up either.

5. A timed practice run

The practice demo has a group of made-up people and saves nothing. Use it a few days before the sale:

  1. Open the demo, and open a notes app or a message to yourself as a stand-in for the booking form.
  2. Start a timer.
  3. Copy and paste each person's registration number and postcode into the note, switching back and forth as you would on the day. The demo group has four people; a real booking can have up to six.
  4. Stop the timer and check every number pasted in full.
  5. Do it again. The second go is almost always much quicker, because the switching becomes automatic.

Never practise on the real See Tickets site, and never with the queue open.

If something goes wrong

  • A box says to tap again. The browser didn't allow the copy. Tap it once more. If it still fails, the number is shown in full on the box, so you can type it in.
  • A name doesn't come up on the booking page. The details may be wrong in the group itself. Don't keep retrying: carry on with the others and check that person in the chat.
  • A friend's link doesn't open. Links stop working after the seasonal clear-by date. Set the group up again and send a new link.
  • Your group has gone. Clearing your browser's data, using a private window or the seasonal clearing will remove it. Open a share link again, or set it up again.

Privacy

The tool runs in your browser. Your group is saved on your phone only, and the tool's code never sends it anywhere. The site is free because it shows Google ads, so Google's ad code also runs on these pages. The code is open source, and a public check compares the live site with the published code every six hours.

Groups and links are cleared on a seasonal schedule, so details don't hang around after ticket season. If you want to delete everything sooner, open How your data is handled in the tool and choose Delete everything on this phone.

What the tool can't do

Glasto Quick Copy can't speed up the queue or get you a better place in it. It doesn't connect to See Tickets and doesn't fill in the booking form for you. What it does is make the minute after you get through calm and accurate, which is the part of ticket day you can control.
