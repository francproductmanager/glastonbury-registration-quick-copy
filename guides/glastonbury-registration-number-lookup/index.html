Lost your Glastonbury registration number? Find or change it

How to get your Glastonbury registration number back with the official lookup, what to do if it finds nothing, how to check a friend's number, and how to change your photo, postcode or email.

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 26 October 2026.

Your registration number is in the email See Tickets sent when your photo was accepted. If you can't find that email, you don't need to register again: the official registration lookup sends your number back to you. This page covers finding it, checking a friend's, and changing the details on your registration.

Find your number with the lookup

  1. Go to the registration lookup on glastonbury.seetickets.com. Start from the festival's own website if you want to be sure you're in the right place.
  2. Enter the email address you registered with.
  3. Open the email that comes back. It has your registration number, and links to view, edit or delete your registration and to update your photo.
  4. Copy the number from that email and send it to whoever's organising your group, as text rather than a screenshot.

If nothing arrives within a few minutes, check your spam and promotions folders before trying again.

If the lookup finds nothing

  • Try every email address you might have used. Many people registered for an earlier festival with a work, university or old personal address. A registration carries over between festivals, so the old one is still the one that counts.
  • Check you were ever accepted. If your photo was rejected and you didn't try again, there's no registration to find.
  • Register again while registration is open. For 2027, registration closed on 25 September 2026, and the festival said there would be a chance to register again before any spring resale. See how to register.

Registered twice?

If you've registered more than once, use the number from your most recent confirmation email, and make sure that's the one your group has. When organising a group, it's worth asking everyone to forward their latest email rather than retype a number they remember.

Checking a friend's number

You can't look up someone else's registration: the lookup only emails the address on it. So ask each friend to run the lookup themselves if they're unsure, then send you the number and postcode from that email. On ticket day, the booking page shows each person's name once you enter their number and postcode, which is your final check. See collecting registration numbers from WhatsApp.

Changing your details

WhatHowWhen
Your photoThe update link in your confirmation emailWhile registration is open, and only before a booking is linked to your registration
Your postcodeThe edit link in your confirmation email, or tell your lead booker the old oneWhile registration is open. What matters is that the postcode typed matches the registration
Your email addressThe edit link in your confirmation emailWhile registration is open. In 2025, changing it once a booking was linked needed a request to See Tickets customer service
Deleting your registrationThe delete link in your confirmation emailAny time you no longer want it

The festival's advice has been to check your registration at least a week before registration closes, so there's time to fix anything.

Once you're booked, your photo is fixed

You can't change your photo while a booking is linked to your registration, and the photo is printed on your ticket. If your appearance changes a lot before June, contact See Tickets customer service for advice rather than assuming it'll be fine at the gate.

Keep your confirmation email in a folder you won't clear out. It's the quickest way to your number next time, and it has the links to change your details.
