Can you change a Glastonbury booking? Address, name and photo

Once you've booked Glastonbury 2027, most details are fixed. How to change your address, what you can add later, and what can't change at all.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 10 November 2026.

6 min read

Once you've booked Glastonbury tickets, most of the details are fixed. The one thing you can change is your delivery address, and you change it with See Tickets, not on your registration. The name and photo on a ticket can't change, a ticket can't be passed to someone else, and a coach booking can't become a general admission one. A few extras, like a car park pass and Refund Protection, can be added when you pay the balance. This guide goes through each, using the rules on the festival's information page for 2027.

Changing your address

This is the change people need most, and the one that's easy to get wrong. The festival says that if you've moved since booking, you need to tell See Tickets, because a new address on your registration won't update the booking. It also says that once you're successful in a sale your registration becomes locked, so any update you try to make there wouldn't be reflected on your booking anyway.

Contact See Tickets through its customer service page, where the festival says you can look up your booking with your booking number and your email address or postcode. Do it well before late May to June 2027, when UK tickets go out. In a group, it's the address for the first name on the booking that counts, unless balances are paid one by one. Everything else about the post is in when do Glastonbury tickets arrive?

Your registration is locked once you book

Before you book, you can update some of your registered details yourself by looking up your registration with your registered email address. The festival says registrations without a ticket booking can be updated, and the changes count in future ticket sales. Once you're booked, your registration locks, and nothing you change there reaches the booking you already have.

So check your details before a sale, not after. How to find your registration and change it while you still can is in finding or changing your registration.

Your photo and your name

The festival says it is not possible to change your registered photo if you have a current booking linked to your registration, and asks you to make sure your photo is up to date before you try to book. The photo is printed on your ticket, and security checks that only the person in the photograph is admitted.

The name can't change either. The festival's terms say a ticket can never be transferred to anyone but the person named on it, and its anti-touting guide says each one is personalised to its holder. For the offers to avoid, see how to spot Glastonbury ticket scams.

If someone in your booking can't go

Nobody can take their place in the booking. Instead, they can leave it: by not paying their balance in 1 to 7 April 2027, or by asking See Tickets for their money back by the end of 7 May 2027, UK time. Either way there's a £25 charge, and the festival says the ticket goes back into the official resale on See Tickets, where a friend can try for it like anyone else. The details are in Glastonbury ticket refunds and the 2027 resale.

Coach bookings

Ticket and coach bookings are the strictest. The festival's coach terms say:

  • A ticket booked with coach travel can't be exchanged for a standard admission ticket, or any other ticket type.
  • Ticket and coach bookings are strictly non-transferable, and you must travel on your chosen coach to get your festival ticket.
  • You can't drop the coach and keep the ticket. If you can't travel on your coach, the whole ticket booking has to go, with an extra £15 coach cancellation fee on top.
  • Coach seats for children 12 and under, and for a personal assistant (PA), should be booked at the same time. If none were left, you contact See Tickets' coach team for children's seats (subject to availability) or the festival's accessibility team for a PA seat.

What you can add later

  • A car park pass, for general admission bookings only: £60, plus £3 postage where bought separately. You can book one when paying the balance, once at least one ticket's balance is paid. See Glastonbury car parking.
  • Refund Protection, which the festival says is only available when making a balance payment or a full ticket booking. So if you want it, add it in the balance window.
  • A campervan ticket, when they go on sale, for general admission bookings. You can't buy one with a ticket and coach booking.

If the person who booked a campervan ticket cancels their own ticket, the campervan ticket is cancelled too. The festival says someone else in the original booking can take it over if they ask at the point of cancellation, for an admin fee, but it can't be passed to someone outside the booking.

Paying the balance: together or separately

The balance is the last point where you shape the booking. The festival says that if you paid deposits for more than one ticket, you can pay the balances together or one by one, and balances can be paid from other cards. Each balance paid on its own becomes a single booking, with its own £11.25 postage and packing, and that person's ticket goes to them. How the balance week works is in deposits and balance payments.

A worked example

Alex books four tickets in October, with Alex as the first name on the booking. In March, Sam moves, and Jo finds out they can't go:

  1. Sam's move doesn't change where the tickets go: unless Sam pays their balance separately, they go to Alex's address. If Alex moved, Alex would need to tell See Tickets, as updating a registration wouldn't reach the booking.
  2. Jo can't hand their place to a friend. Jo simply doesn't pay the balance, and gets the deposit back minus the £25 charge.
  3. The friend who wanted Jo's place tries the spring resale instead.
  4. The three still going decide to pay together, so all three tickets go to Alex, with one postage charge.

What the festival doesn't say

We searched the festival's information page for change, amend, update, transfer and swap. It doesn't say whether you can switch a coach booking to another departure town or day after booking, or what happens if a name or address was typed wrongly at the checkout. For anything not covered here, contact See Tickets through its customer service page, with your booking number to hand.
