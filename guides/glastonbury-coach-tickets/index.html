Glastonbury coach tickets: how ticket and coach packages work

What a ticket and coach package includes, how the coach sale differs from general admission, the rules that catch people out, and coaches without a package.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 9 November 2026.

6 min read

Ticket and coach packages combine a festival ticket with return coach travel from towns and cities around the UK. They're sold in their own sale, a few days before general admission, and they're a popular extra chance to get a ticket. For 2027 that sale has already happened: it opened at 6pm on Thursday 1 October 2026 and sold out in under 30 minutes. Coach packages come with rules that general admission tickets don't have, and a group should agree on them before trying. Already have a general admission ticket and just want a coach there? See coaches without a package.

How the coach sale works

The coach sale runs like the main sale: registration numbers and registered postcodes for everyone, an online queue, and a deposit if you get through. You can book up to six people per transaction. For 2027 the coach sale was at 6pm on Thursday 1 October 2026, three days before general admission.

Once you're through, you choose a departure town and a departure day. For 2027 the day was Wednesday or Thursday. The festival says you must choose one option for your group as a whole: everyone in the booking travels from the same town on the same day. If the option you want isn't available, the festival says to choose a different town or day.

The festival's travel page says its coach partner, National Express, runs services to the festival from over 90 locations.

What a coach package costs

For 2027 the deposit for a coach package was the £100 ticket deposit per person, plus the coach fare and a £3 coach administration fee. The fare depends on the departure town. The rest of the ticket price is paid in the same balance window as general admission, 1 to 7 April 2027. See deposits and balance payments.

Two extras general admission ticket holders can add aren't open to you: the festival says it is not possible to buy a car park pass or a campervan ticket when booking or paying the balance for a ticket and coach booking. The full picture of what Glastonbury 2027 costs is in the 2027 cost guide.

How your tickets reach you

Coach packages don't arrive like general admission tickets, which come in the post. For 2027 the festival says:

  • Your coach travel voucher is expected to be sent as an e-ticket from mid-May.
  • Your festival ticket is handed out on the coach once it has left the pick-up point, on the way to the festival.
  • You need photo ID to receive your festival ticket on the coach. The festival's list of what to bring says the lead booker of a coach package needs original ID documents, not photocopies.
  • Travel on your booked coach is mandatory: it's the only way to get your festival ticket.

The rules that catch people out

  • You must travel on your booked coach. Coach tickets can't be changed to general admission, and bookings can't be transferred.
  • Children need their own coach seat. Children aged 12 and under, including babes in arms, don't need a festival ticket, but they do need a coach seat, booked at the same time as your package. If the seats aren't available when you book, the festival says to email coachtravel@seetickets.com straight after booking to ask about extra seats, which are subject to availability at standard fares.
  • Personal assistants need a seat on the same coach. If you plan to apply for a PA ticket, book their coach seat with your package. If it isn't available, say on your access application that you need an extra coach seat for your PA.
  • No campervans and no parking. Coach ticket holders can't book a campervan or caravan pitch, in the accessible campsite or the general campervan fields, or buy a car park pass.
  • Coaches are full and the toilets are basic. Each coach has at least one toilet, but the festival says they aren't wheelchair accessible and aren't regularly cleaned during the journey. There are no flexible breaks.
  • Wheelchair access isn't guaranteed. The coach terms say See Tickets can't guarantee every coach has wheelchair access and storage. If you need them, email coachtravel@seetickets.com and accessibility@glastofest.co.uk no later than 30 April.

The festival asks everyone to think about whether a long, full coach journey suits their health, mobility and comfort before booking. More on this is in Glastonbury 2027 accessibility and access requirements.

Arriving and leaving

Coaches arrive at the Festival Coach Station, by Pedestrian Gate A. The festival says registered access customers can take an accessible shuttle bus from there to the Access Arrivals Tent, people staying at Worthy View can take the Worthy Shuttle, and there's no festival bus to Sticklinch: you walk there via Pedestrian Gate A.

You travel home on your booked coach too. The coach terms say that, to get people away from the site quickly, coaches may board passengers outside the scheduled departure times if there are seats on other services.

If you can't go

You can't cancel just the coach. The festival says it is not possible to cancel part of the booking: if you can't travel on your coach, you cancel the whole ticket booking. Its refund section says it keeps the £25 ticket charge plus a £15 coach cancellation fee from any cancelled ticket and coach booking, before the 7 May 2027 deadline. The ticket terms on the same page mention a different coach charge, so ask See Tickets which applies. See Glastonbury ticket refunds.

Should your group try the coach sale?

Many groups try the coach sale first as an extra chance, then general admission if it doesn't work. Before you do, agree on these:

  1. One departure point and day. Everyone in a booking travels from the same town on the same day, so check it works for all of them.
  2. What if only some of you get coach tickets? Do the rest still try general admission, and will the coach people still go?
  3. Don't double book. If someone gets coach tickets for the group, nobody should then book the same people in general admission. You'd be left cancelling tickets.
  4. Getting home. You travel back on your booked coach too, so everyone has to be happy leaving at the same time.

How fast coach packages sell out

Coach packages go quickly. NME reported that the 2027 coach packages sold out in under 30 minutes, and the 2025 ones in 32 minutes. The festival says over 22,000 coach and ticket packages for Glastonbury 2025 were sold before the general sale. Earlier years are compared in how fast Glastonbury tickets sell out.

Coach tickets in the resale

Coach packages also come back in the spring resale. In 2025 the coach resale was on a Thursday evening, before the general admission resale on the Sunday, and was limited to two tickets per transaction, compared with four for general admission. Plan resale coach bookings in pairs. See how the spring resale works.

Coaches without a package

If you have a general admission ticket, you can still travel by coach: the festival's travel section lists coaches you book yourself, separately from your festival ticket. The train, local buses and the rest are in how to get to Glastonbury. A coach ticket on its own doesn't get you in: the festival says e-tickets for coach travel or accommodation won't grant entry to the festival by themselves.

  • National Express, the festival's official coach partner, runs direct services from over 90 locations across the UK to the festival. The festival points to the National Express website for times, prices and booking.
  • From Bristol, National Express runs a direct coach between Bristol Bus Station and the festival coach station, booked the same way.
  • From Brighton or London, the registered charity Brighton Peace and Environment Centre (BPEC) sells coach tickets, with all profits going to its work. The festival says its coaches run on the Wednesday.

The festival's travel section doesn't say which year these coach details are for, and says its 2027 bus services are yet to be confirmed, so check times and fares with the coach company before you book. Being dropped off instead? The festival's Drop and Collect point is in Glastonbury car parking.

Getting ready

Have each person's registration number and registered postcode ready to paste, just as for general admission, plus your chosen departure town and day and a backup. The clock is running while you choose a coach.
