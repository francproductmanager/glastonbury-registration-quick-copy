Glastonbury 2027 campervans, Worthy View and tipis

Worthy View, Sticklinch, the new Pylon Field and Holt Farm, campervans and tipis at Glastonbury 2027: tent prices, who can book, parking and sale dates.

JC

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 20 November 2026.

7 min read

Accommodation at the festival's official campsites, where your tent is already put up for you, went on sale at 6pm on Thursday 19 November 2026. When we checked the festival's information page on the morning of 20 November 2026, it didn't say whether any of the campsites had sold out. There are four of these campsites for 2027: Worthy View and Sticklinch, which return, and two new ones, Pylon Field and Holt Farm. You can only book if you already have a valid ticket booking for Glastonbury 2027, and all bookings are taken by See Tickets.

For campervan tickets and tipis, the same page still says bookings open "later in the autumn", and that "the Campervan ticket sale date will be announced soon".

A Glastonbury ticket already includes camping in the general fields: you bring a tent and pitch it (see where to camp at Glastonbury for choosing a field). Everything on this page is an extra, bought separately, and every option needs a festival ticket first.

At Worthy View and Sticklinch you bring your own camping beds and bedding; at Pylon Field and Holt Farm the festival says you can add airbeds or bedding to your booking, or bring your own.

Booking any of the four campsites

The festival's 2027 booking terms for its official campsites say:

  • Everyone staying needs a festival ticket. All people aged 13 or over in the accommodation must have a valid full weekend festival ticket, and you can't check in to the campsite without your ticket.
  • Names when you book. The names of everyone staying must be given when booking, with final confirmation no later than one week before the festival.
  • The booking is in the lead booker's name. It can't be sold or transferred to someone else.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel until 11.59pm on 7 May 2027, with a £25 administration charge per unit. After that there are no refunds unless the whole festival is cancelled. Optional TicketPlan Booking Refund Protection is offered when you book; the festival says it covers the accommodation only if the lead booker can't attend.
  • Pitches are first come, first served. The festival will try to put friends and family together if they book the same type of accommodation, but can't guarantee it.
  • Only the festival's own tents. You can't put up extra tents or gazebos, and campervans and caravans aren't allowed on these campsites.
  • Car parking is booked with the accommodation. The terms say every car needs a parking ticket for your own campsite, bought in advance, and parking can't be booked if you're travelling by coach. A general festival car park pass won't get you into the Worthy View or Sticklinch car parks. See Glastonbury car parking.

If you have a question about a booking, the festival says to contact See Tickets, as the festival can't see its booking system.

Worthy View

Worthy View is the festival's original campsite with tents already up, south of the festival site on Pennard Hill. It has scout tents for 2 to 8 people and the 4 person Glastonbury Bell tent (prices in the table above).

  • Car parking: £70 per vehicle, booked with the accommodation, in car parks in the field next to the campsite. Drivers come in on the red route (A361) from the north or the Brown Route (A37) from the south.
  • The walk in is steep. The footpath between Worthy View and the festival site has steps at a steep gradient, and the festival says it isn't suitable for wheelchairs, prams or child buggies. There's an accessible toilet, but no accessible showers, medicine storage or wheelchair or medical charging.
  • Shuttle: there's no shuttle from Worthy View onto the festival site, but if you arrive by coach or bus you can take the Worthy Shuttle from the Festival Coach Station to the campsite.

Sticklinch

Sticklinch is west of the festival site, on the former West Campervan Fields. It has the same scout tents and Glastonbury Bell tent as Worthy View, at the same prices.

  • Car parking: limited, at £70 per vehicle, booked with the accommodation. If you drive, you arrive first at the Sticklinch car park and the Sticklinch Main Gate. Blue Badge or accessible parking has to be arranged through the festival's Access Team.
  • An easier walk in. The footpath into the festival has a gentle gradient, and the festival says it's accessible for wheelchairs, prams and child buggies. There's a wheelchair accessible toilet and shower, and charging for wheelchairs and essential medical equipment, which you arrange through the Access Team before 30 April 2027.
  • No shuttle. There's no festival bus to Sticklinch: from the Festival Bus Station you walk via Pedestrian Gate A, about 2.3km.

Pylon Field (new for 2027)

Pylon Field is next to Pedestrian Gate B, at the north east corner of the festival site, and the festival says it's a level walk onto the site. There's no shuttle. It has bell tents for up to 4 people and 7m safari bell tents for up to 6. The festival's Pylon Field section lists no car park or parking price.

Holt Farm (new for 2027)

Holt Farm is on the western edge of the festival site, next door to Sticklinch. You walk onto the festival site through the Holt Farm Checkpoint. It has Mongolian style yurts for 2, 4 or 6 people, a 4 person tipi tent and a 4 person bell tent, which the festival says come unadorned to keep prices down.

Drivers follow the Orange route to the car parks Holt Farm shares with Sticklinch, at £70 per vehicle, booked with the accommodation.

Campervans and caravans

Live-in vehicles go in their own fields: a pitch at £225 for a vehicle up to 8m long, in the Chestnut Campervan Fields or at the Bath & West Showground, or a large pitch at £350 for anything longer, only at the Bath & West Showground. Campervan tickets are only for general admission ticket holders: if you booked a ticket and coach package, you can't bring a campervan.

The festival says 2027 campervan and caravan tickets go on sale later in the autumn, and that demand is usually very high, so it recommends booking as soon as they're released. If you're planning one, agree as a group who's booking it before the date is announced.

There's also an accessible campervan field, which in 2025 had sold out by the time of the spring resale. See Glastonbury 2027 accessibility and access requirements.

Tipis

For 2027 the festival's information page lists tipis at £2,000 for five nights, from Wednesday 23 to Sunday 27 June, for up to six adults. The price doesn't include festival tickets, so everyone staying in a tipi needs their own. The festival says tipi bookings will open later in the autumn.

Booking as a group

  • Decide who books what before the sale: one person for the tent, one for the campervan pitch. Two people booking the same thing is easy to do and slow to undo.
  • Check everyone has a ticket first. None of these options gets anyone into the festival on its own, and you need everyone's name when you book a tent.
  • Split the cost per head. A £2,000 tipi shared by six adults is about £333 each, before their festival tickets. See what Glastonbury 2027 costs.
  • Book the festival's own options through See Tickets. The festival says it can't endorse any campsites but its own, and warns about unofficial off-site camping. See ticket scams.
