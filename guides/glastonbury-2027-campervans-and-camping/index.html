Glastonbury 2027 campervans, Worthy View, tipis and parking

The extra places to stay and park at Glastonbury 2027: campervan pitches, Worthy View, tipis and car park passes, with 2027 prices where they're published, who can book, and when they've gone on sale.

By Jonathan Connolly, who books for a group of friends every Glastonbury ticket day and built this site's tool. Facts checked 28 October 2026.

A Glastonbury ticket includes camping in the general fields: you bring a tent and pitch it. Everything on this page is an extra, bought separately through See Tickets, and every option needs a festival ticket first. Most of them sell out, so it's worth knowing the prices and the rules before they go on sale.

Campervans and caravans

Live-in vehicles go in their own fields, with a standard pitch at £225 for vehicles under 8m and a large pitch at £350 for vehicles 8m or longer. Campervan tickets are only for general admission ticket holders: if you booked a ticket and coach package, you can't bring a campervan.

The festival said 2027 campervan and caravan tickets would go on sale later in the autumn. They have sold out quickly in recent years, so if you're planning one, agree as a group who's booking it and have their card ready before the date is announced.

There's also an accessible campervan field, which in 2025 had sold out by the time of the spring resale. See going to Glastonbury with access requirements.

Worthy View

Worthy View is the festival's own campsite on Pennard Hill, south of the main site, with tents already put up for you when you arrive, so you only bring your bags. Bedding isn't provided. It's booked only through See Tickets, and you need a valid 2027 festival ticket to book. The 2027 price hasn't been published yet.

Tipis

For 2027 the festival's information page lists tipis at £2,000 for five nights, from Wednesday 23 to Sunday 27 June, for up to six adults. The price doesn't include festival tickets, so everyone staying in a tipi needs their own.

Car park passes

A car park pass is £60, plus £3 postage where bought separately, and it's sold during the balance window from 1 to 7 April 2027, only to general admission ticket holders. If you're staying at Worthy View, you need its own car parking pass, booked with the accommodation, not a general one. How to buy a pass, friends driving separately to a campervan and the festival's advice on arriving by car are in Glastonbury car parking.

Booking as a group

  • Decide who books what before the sale: one person for the campervan, one for the car park pass. Two people booking the same thing is easy to do and slow to undo.
  • Check everyone has a ticket first. None of these options gets anyone into the festival on its own.
  • Split the cost per head. A £60 car park pass shared four ways is £15 each. See what Glastonbury 2027 costs.
  • Book the festival's own options through See Tickets. Be wary of anyone else offering a pitch in the festival's own fields or a car park pass. See ticket scams.
