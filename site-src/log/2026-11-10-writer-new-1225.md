- Ticket dispatch section: a change of address on your registration won't update your delivery address; tickets go to the first name on the booking unless balances were paid separately; UK tickets sent in late May to June.
- Further information: see your booking on the See Tickets customer service page with your booking reference number and email or postcode.
- Terms, clause 1, and the anti-touting guide: tickets non-transferable to anyone but the named holder; cancelled tickets resold at face value in the official resale.
- Cancellations: unpaid deposits refunded less the admin charge and cancelled; refunds through See Tickets before 7 May 2027 less the admin charge; non-refundable after, unless Refund Protection covers it; Refund Protection only when paying the balance or a full ticket booking.
- Coach sections and coach terms: no exchange to general admission or other ticket types; non-transferable; can't cancel part of the booking; the extra coach cancellation charge; children's and PA seats booked at the time, then the coach team or the accessibility team.
- Car parking and campervan sections: car park passes for general admission only, from the balance once one balance is paid; no campervan ticket with a coach booking; the campervan takeover rule within the original booking.
- Payment processing: balances together or individually, from other cards, separate balances become single bookings with their own postage.
