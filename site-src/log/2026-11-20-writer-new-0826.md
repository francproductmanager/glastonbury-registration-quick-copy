## The guide

`split-glastonbury-ticket-costs`, topic "after", placed after the deposits guide, published today. Title "How to split Glastonbury ticket costs fairly in a group" (55 characters, no search title needed). Summary 153 characters, about 1,000 words.

Every rule is from the festival's info page, downloaded in full today and searched for balance, deposit, postage, separately, individually, collectively, refund, cancel, car park, coach and Refund Protection:

- Tickets: deposit per person at booking, plus the coach fare and a £3 coach administration fee on a ticket and coach booking; postage charged per order of up to six.
- Payment processing: balances paid collectively or individually, from other cards; a balance paid separately becomes a single booking with its own postage; each person is responsible for paying their balance.
- Ticket dispatch: tickets go to the address for the first name on the booking; anyone who paid their balance separately gets their own ticket.
- Car park tickets: bought with the balance, only once one balance on the booking is paid; general admission only.
- Cancellations: an unpaid balance cancels the ticket and refunds the deposit to the card it was booked on, less the admin charge; refunds before the deadline less the admin charge, plus the coach charge on coach bookings; Refund Protection bought when paying the balance, per person.

All prices and dates use facts. The worked numbers are arithmetic from them: six deposits £600; six balances £1,848; with one postage charge £1,859.25; each share £309.88 (the postage share of £1.875 rounded); paying separately £319.25 each, £67.50 postage for the group, £56.25 more in total, £9.38 each; £75 back from an unpaid deposit.

Links in: the deposits guide's "Who pays what in a group" (one clause added to its last sentence) and the cost guide's worked example (one sentence). Dates on both left alone (links only). Updates page: a bullet added at the top of today's entry.
