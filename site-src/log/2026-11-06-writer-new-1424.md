## The guide

Sources, all downloaded in full and searched as text today:
- The festival's history section: the index and every year page it lists (35 years from 1970 to 2025). Each price in the table is from that year's page, and each year in the table links to it. Searched every year page for "£", "price", "ticket" and "fee". The 2013 to 2016 pages have no "£" at all, so the table says no price is given. No year page mentions a booking fee, so the guide says the older prices are the price quoted, not necessarily what buyers paid.
- The 2023 balance reminder (festival news): £285 plus £5 booking fee balance, £9.75 postage (£2.50 coach e-tickets), £25 refunded and £25 kept on an unpaid deposit (so a £50 deposit), one postage fee when balances are paid together.
- Glasto Fest Feed's 2024 sale and balance pages: £355 plus £5, £75 deposit, £280 plus £5 balance, £9.95 postage (£2.75 coach), £50 refunded on an unpaid deposit, resale after 8 April 2024 paid in full at £355 plus £5.
- The 2025 sale FAQ: £378.50 including £5, £75 deposit, £10.25 postage (£2.75 coach).
- The 2027 ticket announcement and the info page: the 2027 facts, what a ticket includes, sold only at glastonbury.seetickets.com, car park and campervan prices, Refund Protection with the balance, the deposit admin charge, resale after 8 April 2027 paid in full, the 2026 fallow year. Searched the info page for "2028": no mention, and the guide says so.
- Fallow years: the 2011 page (2012), the 2019 page (2018), the 2022 page (two year Covid break) and the info page (2026).

Worked numbers are arithmetic from the table: the 2025 balance (£378.50 less £75), deposits for six, and the percentage rises. "The biggest rise from one festival to the next since 1992" compares each listed year with the one before it.
