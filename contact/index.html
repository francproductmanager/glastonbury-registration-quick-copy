Contact

We'd love to hear from you, whether it's a correction, an idea, a bug or a privacy question.

Corrections, ideas and bugs

Open an issue on our public GitHub page. Please don't include real registration numbers or postcodes.

Security problems

Please report security issues privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting rather than in public.

Privacy questions

For questions about your data or this site's cookies, see our privacy and cookies policy, then contact us using the options above.

Ticket questions

We can't help with bookings, refunds or registrations: we're not connected to the festival. Please contact See Tickets customer service or check the official Glastonbury website.
