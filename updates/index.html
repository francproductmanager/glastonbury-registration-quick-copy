Site updates

What's changed on this site and when. We update the guides after every sale and official announcement.

28 October 2026

  • New: Glastonbury ticket refunds: the 2027 refund deadline, the administration charge, what Refund Protection covers and what to do when someone in your group drops out.
  • Updated: the access requirements guide now gives the final deadline for Access Applications listed by the festival, and how to ask for the form. The deposits guide now links to the refund rules.
  • Rechecked against the festival's pages on 28 October 2026: the ticket price, deposit, balance, balance window and festival dates are unchanged.

26 October 2026

  • Guides now have a named author, Jonathan Connolly. See About.
  • New: when is the next Glastonbury ticket sale?, one page we keep up to date for every sale, starting with the 2027 resale and the 2028 sale.
  • Clearer: the 2027 campervan, tipi and car park prices now say they're as listed on the festival's information page on 26 October 2026, with a link, so you can check them before you book. Buying from outside the UK now has its own part of the checkout guide and an FAQ answer.
  • Reorganised after a review of the whole site, so each guide answers one question and each fact has one home:
  • Merged: paying and booking problems are now one checkout guide; the ticket day mistakes are part of the checklist; the two tool guides are now one guide. Old links redirect to the new pages.
  • Renamed and moved: the resale guide is now Glastonbury 2027 resale, with what's known so far for 2027. Buying for a group is now the Glastonbury syndicate guide. What to do after booking now leads with the 2027 balance payment.
  • New: a free spreadsheet template, volunteering at Glastonbury 2027 and what Glastonbury 2027 costs.
  • Expanded: registration now has step by step instructions, when registration reopens and how to change your details; the queue guide explains the queue's screens and errors.
  • Easier to use: calendar files for the balance window and the festival, a chart of sell-out times, a tickable and printable checklist, a copy button on every ready-made message, and screenshots of the tool.
  • Also new: finding or changing your registration, campervans, camping and parking, how common ticket scams are in the scams guide, and a short video in the tool guide.
  • Hear about new dates without signing up: subscribe to the 2027 dates calendar and new dates, like the resale, appear in your calendar app. Or follow this page in a feed reader with its feed.
  • In the tool: groups of five or six now show a note that resale bookings have been limited to four people, and a message like "here's mine! Sam..." now picks out Sam.
  • Six new guides with tips for the current ticket system: how to get Glastonbury tickets, your first go at Glastonbury tickets, setting up your phone or laptop, what to do if checkout fails, collecting registration numbers from WhatsApp and using Glasto Quick Copy at the front of the queue.
  • Updated: the ticket day checklist and how the queue works now follow the official 2027 sale FAQ: be in the waiting room at least 10 minutes before the sale, not just a few minutes.
  • A new FAQ answer on whether there's a trick to getting tickets.
  • Corrections after a full fact check of every guide: 2024 tickets cost £360 including the booking fee, with a £75 deposit (we had £355), so the 2024 to 2025 price rise was about 5%, not 7%. The 10 minutes at the front of recent sales was for the page where you enter registration details; payment has had its own, shorter timer. The rule that registrations could be locked for 10 minutes comes from the 2025 resale. Random places in the queue still apply when a sale opens, not just in earlier sales. The guides now say the payee name "The Way Ahead Group" can appear when paying with Apple Pay or Google Pay. The savings tip is now the same in every guide (about £52 a month), and the cancellation cut-off no longer gives a date we couldn't confirm.
  • Every guide rechecked against its sources on 26 October 2026.
