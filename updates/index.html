Site updates

What's changed on this site and when. We update the guides after every sale and official announcement.

20 October 2026

  • Ten new guides: 2027 key dates, recent sales compared, what to do after booking, paying for tickets, coach tickets, access requirements, children and teenagers, other ways to get to Glastonbury, ticket scams and a glossary.
  • Ready-to-send messages for your group chat, from collecting details to paying each other back: messages to send your group.
  • Updated for the 2027 sale results: sell-out times, the new queue, the April 2027 balance window and postage costs.
  • Corrections: the resale allowed up to four general admission tickets per booking in 2025, not two (two was the limit for coach packages). Refund and cancellation terms now say which year they come from.
  • Every existing guide expanded with worked examples, group plans and troubleshooting.
  • Guides grouped by topic on the guides page.
  • The tool's working screens no longer show ads. Ads appear only on the home page and the guides and information pages.

6 October 2026

  • Guides, FAQ and information pages published alongside the tool.
  • The tool learned to read WhatsApp chats where details are on separate lines.
  • Share links number repeated first names instead of including surnames.
  • Groups and links are cleared on a seasonal schedule.
  • A visit counter on the home page.

5 October 2026

  • A redesigned tool with a practice demo.
  • A public check that the live site matches the published code, every six hours.
