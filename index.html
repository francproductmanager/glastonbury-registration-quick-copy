Get your group's Glastonbury details ready before ticket day.

Save everyone's registration number and postcode on one page. On the day, tap to copy each one.

What this is

Glasto Quick Copy is a free, unofficial helper for the Glastonbury ticket sale. It puts everyone's registration number and postcode on one page, so when you get through the queue you can tap to copy each one and paste it into the booking form.

Why this exists

When you get through the Glastonbury queue, the lead booker has to enter a registration number and registered postcode for every person in the booking, up to six people. That's sixty digits and six postcodes, typed under pressure, usually while scrolling a group chat to find who sent what three weeks ago. One wrong digit and that friend isn't added.

This tool was made by a product manager who did exactly that scrolling every ticket day and wanted the minute after the queue to be calm. It can't get you through the queue faster. It makes sure nothing slows you down once you're in.

What it does

  • Reads your group chat for you. Paste the messages where friends sent their details, from WhatsApp or anywhere else, and it picks out names, registration numbers and UK postcodes. You check and fix each person before saving. The pasted text is never stored.
  • Flags gaps early. Anyone missing a postcode is highlighted, so you can chase them days before the sale, not during it.
  • Shares one page with the group. Send everyone a link. Whoever gets through the queue first opens it and books for the others, so it doesn't matter who wins the race.
  • Makes ticket day one tap per box. Tap a number to copy it, paste it into the booking form, and the next box lights up. A progress bar shows who's done, and Keep screen on stops your phone locking while you book.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't register you, buy tickets, join the queue for you or talk to the ticket site in any way. You still register and book on the official Glastonbury and See Tickets websites. It's simply the quickest way to have the right details in your hand when it matters.

Your details stay on your phone

There are no accounts and no database. Your group is saved in your own browser, and the tool's code never sends it anywhere. Share links carry first names, numbers and postcodes in the part of the link that browsers don't send to servers, and surnames are left out. Everything is cleared automatically after each ticket season. The code is open source and a public check compares the live site with it every six hours.
